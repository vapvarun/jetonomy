# How-To Guides

Step-by-step recipes for the things community owners do most. Each guide starts from a goal, gives you the exact clicks, and ends with a quick check that it worked. You do not need any coding.

New to Jetonomy? Start with [Try It With Demo Content](07-try-it-with-demo-content.md). It fills your site with a sample community you can explore and then remove in one click.

## Set up your community

| I want to... | Guide |
|---|---|
| See how everything works before I build my own | [Try It With Demo Content](07-try-it-with-demo-content.md) |
| Help customers with product questions | [Launch a Support Forum](01-launch-a-support-forum.md) |
| Collect feature requests and share what is planned | [Collect Feature Requests and Show a Roadmap](04-collect-feature-requests-and-show-a-roadmap.md) |
| Put the community in my site menu or on a page | [Add the Community to Your Site](06-add-the-community-to-your-site.md) |
| Make it look like the rest of my site | [Match Your Brand](10-match-your-brand.md) |
| Bring my old forum across | [Move From bbPress, wpForo or Asgaros](12-move-from-bbpress-or-wpforo.md) |
